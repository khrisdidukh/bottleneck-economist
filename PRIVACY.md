# Bottleneck Economist: privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Bottleneck Economist is a Forge app for Jira Cloud by Khrystyna Didukh (Vienna, Austria). The Atlassian Marketplace listing is published by Lumos Strategies OÜ (Estonia).

**What the app reads.** Work items of the Jira projects you analyse (keys, status changes with timestamps, resolution, issue type) through the Jira REST API, with the permissions you grant on installation.

**What the app stores.** Aggregate flow figures, the generated report, the keys of work items currently waiting at the bottleneck, your money assumptions and workflow setup. Everything is stored in Atlassian's Forge storage on your own site and processed by Forge functions hosted by Atlassian. Names, account IDs, e-mail addresses and the text of work items are not stored.

**Where data goes.** Nowhere outside Atlassian. The app has no external egress. If a site administrator connects the optional Teamwork Graph connector, the report (aggregate figures and work item keys) is written to your organisation's Atlassian Teamwork Graph, where it is visible to all users of the workspace.

**Retention and deletion.** Uninstalling the app deletes its Forge storage. Disconnecting the Teamwork Graph connector removes the reports from the Teamwork Graph.

**Personal data.** The app does not store personal data. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/khrisdidukh/bottleneck-economist/issues.
